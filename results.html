2027 World Sailing Championships — Fortaleza, Brazil ↗

January 22–30, 2027

I qualified for this event through the 2026 Senior World Championships. Fortaleza hosts the one-person events of the 2027 World Sailing Championships, including the women’s dinghy (ILCA 6), and it’s also an Olympic qualifier: 15 country quota places for the LA 2028 Olympic Games are up for grabs.

56 Trofeo SAR Princesa Sofia — Palma de Mallorca

March 26 – April 3, 2027

This is the individual qualifier for the Pan American Games: the top Canadian sailor goes on to represent Canada at the Pan Am Games. It’s held in Palma de Mallorca and is part of the Sailing Grand Slam series. The 2026 Trofeo Princesa Sofia Regatta hosted a total of 62 countries and over 1,000 sailors across all 10 Olympic classes. In my opinion, one of the most exciting events of the year!
